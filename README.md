# CollageStudio - Aesthetic Multi-Card Story Generator

An editorial story & collage generator inspired by **Unfold**, designed to take **15–30+ photos** and automatically organize them into a magazine-grade multi-card story series.

---

## 🚀 How to Launch

You can open the app in two ways:

1. **Option A (Instant)**:
   Double-click `index.html` in the `CollageGenerator` folder to open directly in Safari, Chrome, or any modern browser.

2. **Option B (One-Click Local Server)**:
   Double-click `start.command` on your Desktop to start a local server and open `http://localhost:8080`.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Automatic Multi-Card Partitioning**:
  Drop 15–30 photos at once. The engine automatically chunks them into 3–5 photos per slide with diverse editorial layouts (horizontal strips, 2x2 grids, hero + thumbnail accents, editorial text cards).
- **Aesthetic Themes (like Unfold)**:
  - **Clasico**: Clean white editorial borders with classic museum serif typography.
  - **Paper**: Warm cream craft paper vibe with subtle grain texture.
  - **Noir Studio**: Deep matte obsidian background with high-contrast frames.
  - **Film 35mm**: Analog frame styling with date stamps & vintage codes.
  - **Warm Terra**: Earthy terracotta tones.
  - **Minimalist**: Borderless full-bleed high-fashion styling.
- **Multiple Story Formats**:
  - `9:16` Instagram / TikTok Story (1080×1920)
  - `4:5` Instagram Portrait Post (1080×1350)
  - `1:1` Square Feed (1080×1080)
  - `16:9` Landscape / Desktop (1920×1080)
- **Live Editorial Customization**:
  - Tweak photo gaps, outer padding, and corner radius in real-time.
  - Customizable serif subheader, title headline, and journal paragraph.
  - 1-click layout shuffle per card or across all cards.
- **High-Res Export**:
  - **Export All Cards (ZIP)**: Generates crystal-clear PNG files for all slides in one download.
  - **Export This Card**: Download individual cards instantly.
  - Supports **Ultra Crisp 2X Retina** (2160×3840px) export quality.
- **Instant Demo**:
  Includes a built-in "Demo Photos" button that preloads 20 aesthetic landscape & portrait scenes so you can test and experiment immediately.
